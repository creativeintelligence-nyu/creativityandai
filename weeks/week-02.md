## Readings

### Required

- [Kahneman, Daniel (2011). Two Systems. Thinking, Fast and Slow. New York, Farrar, Straus and Giroux: pp. 18–30.](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Jk9DTDrt4HNcoHbaWgVFo79zAPvTHEoj/view?usp=sharing) **Only read chapters 3 & 4: The Lazy Controller & The Associative Machine.**
- [Sawyer, R. Keith and Danah Henriksen (2023). Explaining Creativity: The Science of Human Innovation, Oxford University Press.](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9780199838202_A23584368/preview-9780199838202_A23584368.pdf) **Only read Part 1: Chapters 1 & 2.**

Cambridge University Press: pp. 148–172.](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9781316993033_A37436424/preview-9781316993033_A37436424.pdf) *Very theory-heavy; only recommended if you're looking for a specific concept.*
