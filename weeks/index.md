# Weekly Modules

Fourteen weekly modules, Thursdays 2:00 to 3:15 PM (online). Each page lists the topics, readings, reading reflection, and assignments for that week.

{: .note }
All assignments and reading reflections are due 24 hours before the next class (2 PM on Wednesdays), unless a specific date is shown.
