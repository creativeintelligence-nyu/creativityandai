## Assignment 1

**Due: Jan 28, 2026, 2 PM**

**Step 1 (started in class):** Create a simple web tool that solves one daily challenge/task/practice for you, or someone in your life. Use a vibe coding platform. We used [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/app) in class. You can also use [Replit](https://replit.com/) (more robust in what you can make), [Cursor](https://cursor.com/) (desktop based), [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) (easy), or [Figma Make](https://www.figma.com/make/) (designers like it). Make a screen recording of your tool, and link it in the assignment along with a few words of what it's doing, what's working/not working, and what you'll change.

**Step 2:** List all the humans you believe made this creative process possible. For example: you, AI developers, AI company owners, dataset curators, game designers who provide the initial dataset, graphic designers whose datasets are used in training the visual design, the intended user of your tool, etc. Then reflect on how this creative *act* could potentially impact these many stakeholders (positively, negatively, economically, creatively, otherwise).
