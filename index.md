## Course Description
Are modern AI systems capable of true creativity or are they simply stochastic parrots, unthinkingly repeating sequences in their training data? We begin with an examination of human creativity and the science of human cognition, followed by an accessible review of the inner workings of modern generative transformer models with a focus on pattern recognition and creative pattern expansion. The course concludes with group or individual projects applying new AI knowledge and skills to creative tasks.

#### Assignment 1 (DUE Jan 28, 2026, 2 PM): 

**Step 1 (started in-class):** Create a simple web tool that solves one daily challenge/task/practice for you, or someone in your life. Use a vibe coding platform. We used Gemini in class. You can also use Replit (more robust in what you can make), Cursor (desktop based), ChatGPT (easy), or Figma make (designers like it). Make a screen recording of your tool, and link it in the assignment along with a few words of what it’s doing, what’s working/not working, what you’ll change. 

**Step 2:** List all the humans you believe made this creative process possible. E.g. you, AI developers, an AI company owners, dataset curators, game designers who provide the initial dataset, graphic designers whose datasets are used in training the visual design, intended user of your tool, etc. Then reflect on how this creative act could potentially impact these many stakeholders (positively, negatively, economically, creatively, otherwise).
